Supplier Review

UK Research Supplier Checklist

3 min read  •  Sourcing

Choosing a supplier for laboratory research compounds is less about finding the lowest price and more about verifying that a business can consistently deliver identity, purity, and documentation you can trust. Because research peptides aren't sold through the same regulated retail channels as consumer goods, purchasers carry more of the diligence burden themselves. This checklist walks through the categories worth reviewing before placing an order with any supplier, our own catalog included.

1. Supplier identity and transparency

A legitimate research supplier should be easy to identify: a registered business name, a physical fulfilment location or jurisdiction, and a working contact channel that responds to pre-sale questions. Anonymous storefronts with no verifiable business details, no returns process, and no way to reach a human are a common red flag across this industry.

2. Analytical documentation

Ask whether batch-specific Certificates of Analysis (COAs) are available, and how they're generated. Third-party HPLC and mass spectrometry testing, tied to a specific lot number rather than a generic "class average," gives you something concrete to evaluate rather than a marketing claim.

3. Storage and handling disclosure

Peptides are sensitive to temperature, light, and moisture. A supplier who documents how product is stored prior to shipment, and what cold-chain measures (if any) apply during transit, is giving you information that directly affects whether the material you receive matches its documentation.

4. Policy clarity

Shipping timelines, order tracking, return/replacement policy for damaged shipments, and a clearly stated research-use-only position should all be published somewhere on the site — not something you have to ask for after paying.

5. Support responsiveness

A quick pre-sale question is a reasonable way to test a supplier's support process. Response time and clarity of answer often correlate with how a supplier will handle a genuine post-sale issue.
